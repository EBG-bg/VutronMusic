# Vutron Music - Mobile use policy

This is an independent open-source mobile adaptation maintained by EBG-bg and contributors. It is not an official NetEase Cloud Music application. This guidance does not replace any component license or applicable law.

Use the app with accounts, recordings and local files you are entitled to access, and follow the relevant provider's service terms and regional restrictions. A returned URL or a successful alternative-source match is a technical result, not proof that you have permission to copy, redistribute or make a recording publicly available. No service-provider authorization, subscription entitlement or music-copyright license is supplied with this software.

Do not upload account cookies, private playlists, audio exports or personal device logs to the public repository. The maintainer's personal test packages and data are not public release assets.

The existing source licenses, copyright notices and warranty disclaimers remain in effect. The original MIT text is preserved in `LICENSE`; the embedded matcher remains LGPL-3.0-only and other dependencies retain their stated licenses. This policy does not prohibit modification, redistribution permitted by those licenses, or reverse engineering required to debug changes to the LGPL component. It creates no additional commercial-use restriction on the standard MIT license text. Upstream's separate README wording is preserved and explained in `REDISTRIBUTION.md`.

Report bugs against the public mobile fork with the app version, Android version and a minimal reproduction. Remove credentials, signed media URLs and personal files from screenshots or attachments before publishing them.
